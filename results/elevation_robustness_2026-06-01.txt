Literature calibration:

  - Elder-fraud literature emphasizes low digital literacy / isolation / cognition -> higher SUSCEPTIBILITY;
    population-level realized-LOSS by digital engagement is under-studied. Our inversion distinguishes
    exposure/capacity from susceptibility (routine-activity reading).

TWO-CHANNEL THEORY OF AI-ERA FRAUD LITERACY (Daniel, 2026-06-01):
  'Digital literacy' conflates two opposing channels:
   Axis 1 = operational/medium literacy (smartphone, apps, transactions) -> EXPOSURE & capacity -> victimization UP  [CONFIRMED here, monotone]
   Axis 2 = AI-threat literacy (deepfake/deepvoice/AI-deception awareness) -> DETECTION -> victimization DOWN       [UNMEASURED: absent in 노인실태조사]
  Net risk = inverted-U / saddle, peaking at "operationally active but AI-naive" elder.
  - Resolves the conventional("low literacy = vulnerable") vs empirical("engagement -> loss") contradiction.
  - Explains Paper G's simulated non-monotonicity (DL Medium 15.75 > Low 12.75 trust).
  - Measurement gap = contribution: no existing survey measures AI-threat literacy -> candidate next KVRB
    attribute / new instrument (on-brand: KVRB adds attributes surveys lack; 치안AI deepfake era).
