KVRB Route-B step B2 — observed PV x RP joint (KCVS 2018): results (2026-05-31)
Builder: src/build_anchor_kcvs_joint.py -> config/anchors/kcvs_joint_b2.csv
Sampler: src/enrich_stage1.py --b2  (draws the nested 3-outcome joint per age x sex x edu cell)

WHAT B2 DOES
  Replaces independent sampling of prior_victimization (PV) and reporting_propensity (RP) with a draw
  from the empirical KCVS joint. RP is defined only for victims (report-to-police item c24), so the
  joint is a nested 3-outcome distribution per cell:
    nonvictim / victim_unreported / victim_reported
  This (a) preserves the real covariance KCVS measured (victimization rate x conditional report rate)
  and (b) fixes a coherence bug: independent sampling had assigned RP to non-victims, for whom the
  construct is undefined.

JOINT ANCHOR (N=12,601 KCVS respondents; 18/20 cells at full age+sex+edu, 2 at age+sex)
  overall: nonvictim 96.2%, victim_unreported 2.9%, victim_reported 0.9%; report-rate|victim 23.4%.

REALIZED IN 1M (--b2)
  victimization rate 3.67% ; report-rate among victims 22.9% (matches observed).
  COHERENCE: RP = -1 (N/A) for all 963,350 non-victims; RP in {0,1} only for the 36,650 victims.
             violations: PV0&RP!=-1 = 0 ; PV1&RP==-1 = 0.
  F6' JOINT FIDELITY: synthetic vs observed KCVS joint, per-cell TVD max 0.0019, mean 0.0008
                      -> the dataset reproduces the observed within-survey joint by construction.

SCOPE (honest)
  PV x RP is the ONE observable within-survey joint (B0). AD x SI is NOT observable (disjoint KGSS
  waves) and stays at the B1 + B3 (conditional-independence) level. Cross-block joints remain
  shared-demographic conditioning (B1) + stated conditional independence (B3). B2 therefore adds
  exactly one measured joint, and the dataset is explicit about it.
