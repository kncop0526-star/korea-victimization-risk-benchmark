KVRB C1 — out-of-sample external validity (2026-05-31)
Question (reviewer C1): predict ONE statistic the dataset never ingested.

INGESTED:  digital_literacy is anchored on the 디지털정보격차 실태조사 활용역량 (Q8 competency items).
NOT INGESTED: internet-use RATE by age (인터넷이용실태조사 / Survey on Internet Use, MSIT-NIA/KISA).
              Different instrument, different national survey, different construct (use vs competency).

TEST: does KVRB's digital-competency gradient by age predict the external internet-use-rate gradient?

KVRB digital_literacy by age band (1M):
  % high (level 4-5):  19-29 67.1 | 30-44 59.7 | 45-59 28.9 | 60-74 6.7 | 75+ 5.3
  % low  (level 1-2):  19-29 6.9  | 30-44 10.8 | 45-59 37.4 | 60-74 78.1 | 75+ 93.6
  mean level:          3.98 / 3.77 / 2.93 / 1.83 / 1.31
External internet-use rate by age (인터넷이용실태조사; ~99% 10s-50s, 60s ~94%, 70+ ~64% in 2023):
  mapped to KVRB bands (approx): 99.9 / 99.9 / 98.5 / 85.0 / 52.0

RESULT
  Spearman rank correlation (KVRB %high-DL vs external use-rate) = 0.975 (p = 0.005).
  Both show the elderly cliff: KVRB %high-DL 28.9 -> 5.3 across 45-59 -> 75+; external 98.5 -> 52.0.
  => Convergent validity across two independent instruments: the competency-anchored attribute, which
     never saw usage rates, rank-predicts the externally measured age gradient of internet use. This
     converts the §4.1 fidelity check from a sampler-check into an out-of-sample validation.

CAVEATS (honest)
  - The external per-band figures are approximate (published series give 60세이상 81.1%, 70대이상 64.4%
    for 2023, ~99% for 10s-50s); the test is RANK-based and robust to the exact elderly values.
  - Convergent validity (competency predicts use), not identity; Pearson r=0.74 is lower than the rank
    rho because the two scales are not linearly comparable. Rank agreement is the correct, near-perfect
    metric here.
